How selling and buying works

Two sides, one marketplace. Artisans list with their voice; buyers search, sample, and place bulk or retail orders with escrow-protected payment.

For the artisan (seller)

SELLER JOURNEY — 5 STEPS

  1. Create the seller profileName, cluster, craft, languages, bank/UPI and Aadhaar-based verification.
  2. Photograph and speakOne phone photo plus a spoken note in any Indian language — the AI writes the listing.
  3. Confirm the AI priceMaterial cost and hours in, a fair market range out; the artisan accepts or edits it.
  4. Publish with a story videoThe cultural story reel is attached to the listing and shared to social channels.
  5. Accept orders, ship, get paidBuyer pays into escrow; payment releases to the artisan's UPI on delivery.
